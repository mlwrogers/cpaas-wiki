## Banned Words and Phrases

For brand consistency, professionalism, and clarity — avoid the following words and phrases:

---

### ❌ Forbidden Adjectives and Buzzwords

Do NOT use:

accordingly, certainly, indeed, hence, innovative, seamless, transformation, synergy, leverage, facilitate, vital, robust, exemplary, optimization, augment, embark, integration, institution, realm, vibrant, efficiency

---

### ❌ Forbidden Phrases

Do NOT use:

"in conclusion", "a testament to", "it’s worth noting", "notwithstanding", "thought-provoking", "ever-evolving", "delve into", "transformative", "it's important to consider"

---

### ✅ Use Instead

Use language that is:

- Simple and plain-spoken  
- Confident, but not overblown  
- Clear, outcome-driven, and benefit-first  
- Consistent with 8x8 brand voice and tone
